PUMP MAINTENANCE MANUAL
Model: Pump P-999

1. INTRODUCTION
This manual covers the operation and maintenance of Pump P-999, designed for industrial fluid transfer.

2. INSPECTION SCHEDULE
- Daily: Check for unusual noise or leaks.
- Weekly: Monitor operating pressure.
- Monthly: Pump bearings must be inspected every month for wear and proper lubrication.
- Annually: Full teardown and internal inspection.

3. TROUBLESHOOTING
Vibration:
Why is the pump vibrating? Common causes include:
- Misalignment between the pump and motor.
- Worn or damaged bearings.
- Loose mounting bolts.

Loss of Pressure:
- Worn wear rings.
- Clogged suction strainer.
- Air leak in the suction line.
