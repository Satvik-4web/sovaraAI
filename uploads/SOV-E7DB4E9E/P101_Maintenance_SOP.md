# P-101 Maintenance SOP
**Equipment**: P-101 (Centrifugal Pump)
**Unit**: Unit-100

## 1. Routine Inspection
Check pump vibration levels every 30 days.

## 2. Abnormal Vibration Response
If vibration exceeds 5.0 mm/s:
- Check alignment and bearing lubrication.
- Prepare an Engineering Approval Note.
- Recommend immediate offline inspection.

## 3. Maintenance Contact
For critical issues, contact Unit-100 Reliability Engineer.
